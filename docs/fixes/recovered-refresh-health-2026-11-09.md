# Clear recovered campaign failures from operational health

The 20:00 Amsterdam refresh on 9 November completed for all three live campaigns. Batch 1 Provider Check lost its first work claim during deployment overlap and completed on automatic retry at 19:07:39 UTC. Its durable job was completed, but the recovery batch never reconciled the previous campaign failure in process-local health. `/health/scheduler` and `check-hosted-health.ts` therefore continued reporting `RECORDED_JOB_FAILURE`.

The correction is confined to `recoverInterruptedGA4Refreshes` in `server/ga4-daily-scheduler.ts`. At the end of the owning batch, a campaign's known aligned-publication failure is removed only when the existing pipeline's completed set proves full publication, alert stages, and durable completion. Other failed campaigns remain flagged; new failures in the same recovery batch are retained. Only an empty remaining failure list clears the matching aligned-publication error. Unattributed errors are not cleared. The historical error timestamp and logs remain available.

The traced path is scheduled owned pipeline -> campaign failure collection -> recovery discovery and claim -> complete publication -> process-local scheduler status -> `/health/scheduler` -> existing monitor. Initial imports and suppressed-alert work do not enter the full-publication completed set and cannot clear the prior failure. No provider requests, persistence, financial calculations, source selections, scheduling times, or monitor criteria change. No persisted-data cleanup is needed.

Regression coverage exercises successful recovery, partial recovery across batches, unrelated campaign success, provider failure, alert failure, ownership/completion failure, suppressed alerts, a new failed campaign in the same batch, and unattributed discovery errors. Existing ownership, stop/restart, source-failure, timing, and monitor tests cover adjacent behavior. Database and provider boundaries in these tests are fixtures. Hosted deployment and final read-only health evidence are recorded separately; this fix is not a certification of unrelated metrics or reports.
